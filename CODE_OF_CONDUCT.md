# Code of conduct

AEON follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
This is the short form; the linked text is the full one.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in AEON — issues, pull requests, discussions — to be free of
harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, and respect differing opinions and experience.
- Give and take constructive feedback; own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Put what is best for the people using AEON ahead of winning an argument.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their
  permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every AEON space on GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews)
and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

AEON has one maintainer. To report a problem:

- **Privately:** open the repository's **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**
  (<https://github.com/cgomez1365/AEON/security/advisories/new>) and start the title with
  "Conduct:". It is not public: only you and the maintainer can see it.
- **Publicly:** reply on the issue or pull request where it happened. Anyone can read it,
  so do not include anyone's private information.

The maintainer respects the privacy and security of whoever reports.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban
anyone who does, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction,
warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
